1. CHECK_IN in capitals
Found 181 rows with event_type = 'CHECK_IN' instead of 'check_in'.
All from one device ---> D04-IN (Riverwalk, Austin).
Broke it down by local day ---> Mon Apr 8 to Sun Apr 14 it's only caps before and after only lowercase. 
So the device switched for exactly one week and then went back.
Daily numbers that week look normal (18-35), so nothing's missing. It's just labeled wrong.
My guess is a firmware or config change that got rolled back.
Anything that matches 'check_in' exactly will lose that whole week for Austin.

2. UTC vs local
Some 2024 check_ins show up as 2025-01-01 in UTC. Converted to local they're Dec 31 evening.
Also saw it when I filtered from Apr 5 UTC and got Apr 4 rows back for Austin.
Need to do all date logic in the branch's timezone.

3. check_in vs check_out gap
74,094 check_ins (incl. the 181 caps ones) vs 73,205 check_outs, so 889 more check_ins.

4. Duplicates
3,736 source_refs show up more than once. 3,756 extra rows in total.
Looked at a few by hand and they're not all the same kind of problem:
- 700 have the same event_ts and were ingested a few seconds apart. Probably a retry.
- 3036 have a different event_ts on the second copy. Every one of those later copies is on either 
Mar 20 (1,224) or Oct 20 (1,812). Looks like two bulk re-sends that stamped the re-send time instead of the real time.
At first I thought fractional seconds might mark the re-sent rows, but they show up every day (150-250/day), 
so that doesn't work. Only the two spike days stand out.
For the report: dedupe on source_ref alone, keep the first copy.
Some refs have 3+ copies, should check how many.

4b. Where the resends came from
It's every device at every branch, in both batches. So, it's not one broken device, something central sent everything again.
Mar 20 07:11 to 07:18 UTC. Oct 20 07:21 to 07:32 UTC. Only a few minutes each time.
That's the middle of the night at every branch, all of them closed. Probably a scheduled job. 
D08 is only in the October batch. That makes sense, since Pearl District opened in May.
The check_in and check_out numbers are almost the same for each device, so whole visits got copied.
That's about 584 duplicate visits on Mar 20 and 874 on Oct 20 if we don't remove duplicates.

4c. Each resend copied the few days right before it.
Mar 20 batch ---> originals from Mar 17 morning to Mar 20 early morning, about 3 days.
Oct 20 batch ---> originals from Oct 16 morning to Oct 20 early morning, about 4 days.
So it's not a monthly job. Looks more like the systems had a problem, then resent its recent backlog
and put the resend time on everything.
The counts are close to a normal 3 or 4 days of events, so it's probably almost all of them.

5.check_in vs check_out after dedupe
After removing duplicates and counting CHECK_IN as check_in:
72,305 unique check_ins vs 71,413 unique don't explain it. These look like real missing check_outs.
Sanity check ---> Rows removed by dedupe add up to 3,756 same as before.

5b. Pairing check_ins with check_outs
Rule I'm using: Line up each member's check_ins and check_outs by time. After a check_in, the next event
should be a check_out at the same branch.
72,305 check_ins (matches the dedupe count).
1,255 have no check_out right after them.
60 have a check_out next, but at a different branch. Not a valid visit per the README.
The 892 gap was hiding things. If 1,255 check_ins have no check_out, then roughly 363 check_outs must have
no check_in before them. So we have two problems at this point.

6. Missing check_outs at Mission Bay (branch 7)
Most branches have about 1-2% of check_ins with no check_out. Probably normal stuff, people tailgating or 
the reader missing a card.
Mission Bay is at 4.3% about 300 more than the others would suggest.
By month, most of the year is fine, about 10 a month. Summer is where it goes wrong: 
103 in June, 146 in July, 84 in Aug.
So it's a summer problem. But even in July most check_ins still have a check_out, so the exit turnstile 
didn't die, it was missing some of them. 

6b. Looked at it by week to find the start and end.
Starts the week of Jun 3. Ends after the week of Aug 5, back to 2 the week of Aug 12. So about 10 weeks. 
The week of July 29 is the worst, 77. Check_ins stay normal the whole time (160-190 a week) so people kept coming, 
only the check_outs went missing.

7. check_outs at a different branch
58 of the 60 are a check_out at another branch just 2 seconds to 2 minutes after the check_in.
Not physically possible, and way too short for a real visit.
Always nearby braches in the same or next timezone (1-2, 3-4, 7-8), never coast to coast.
Not sure why yet. Could be card sharing, wrong member_id, or wrong device/branch on the event.
The other 2 took 1-2 days and both endede at D07-OUT. Probably just the missing check_out problem showing up.
Update: after leaving out the test cards (see 8), only 2 of these are left. The other 58 were the test cards
moving between branches a few minutes apart. So this is really part of problem 8.

8. Two numbers that don't exits
828 events belong to member_ids that aren't in the members table. Only two ids: 990001 and 990002.
They only have check_ins and check_outs, 414 of each. No membership events.
9900001 goes to branches 1-4, 990002 goes to branches 5-8.
About 54 visits per branch, so once a week. Pearl District has 35 because it opened in May.
Always right when the branch opens, and each visit is about a minute.
These look like test cards for checking the turnstiles. They should not count as visits.
Other checks were clean: no missing member_id, no unknown devices, no device at the wrong branch.

9. Late night check_ins at Mission Bay
172 check_ins at branch 7 after closing, all between 11pm and 1am, all in June to August.
Same branch and same months as the missing check_outs. Probably the same problem. 81 different members, 
so it's not one or two staff people.
131 of them do have a check_out next, but it comes 6 hours to 14 days later. Median is about 2 days.
So those aren't real visits. My pairing rule was matching them with a check_out from a later visit.
I can't tell the exact cause from the data. Something was wrong with the turnstiles at Mission Bay that summer.
Need to ask the access-control team.
Fix for the report: a check_out has to be on the same local day as the check_in.

10. Friend visits without the member
58 of 6,875 friend_visits have no check_in by the member at the same branch on the same local day.
The README says a friend can only come in with the member, so these break the rule. 
Small number. Probably the member's check_in just didn't get recorded.

11. Friend visit limits
71 member_months went over the friend limit. basic 41, standard 20, premium 10.
Worst cases: 5 in a month on basic (limit 2), 11 on premium (limit 8).
The data is probably fine here. Looks like the front desk isn't enforcing the limit. 
18 member-months have friend visits but no tier yet, like the member brought friends before 
their membership started.
For tier I used the latest tier event up to the end of that month. If the tier changes mid-month the README
doesn't say which one counts.

12. members.status vs membership events
The README says membership state comes from events, so I compared the two. Most match.
But:
34 are "active" in members, but their last event is a cancellation.
15 are "cancelled" in members, but they started or reactivated after that.
2 are "active" but have no membership events at all.
5 have status "Active" with a capital A. Same kind of problem as CHECK_IN.
So the members table is out of date. Anything that uses members.status for active counts will be off by about 49.

13. Mission Bay check_outs on a different day
When I ran the report logic, Mission Bay lost 1,654 check_ins. Other branches lose about 2%.
1,195 of those have a check_out next, but on a different day. Only at Mission Bay, only June to August.
Gap is 6 hours to 42 days, median about 2 days. Not a clean 24h shift, so it's not just a wrong date.
Looks like both the member's check_out and their next check_in went missing, so my rule paired the 
first check_in with the second visit's check_out.
So it's not only the exit turnstile. Both turnstiles at Mission Bay were losing events that summer. 
6, 9 and this are the same problem. The same-day rule is right to drop these, but Mission Bay's summer
visits will be undercounted and there's no honest way to fill them back in.

14. The real cause at Mission Bay: entrance clock 3 hours ahead
The test for ingested_at < event_ts failed with 1,542 rows. All from D07-IN.
event_ts is always 2h66m to 3h ahead of ingested_at. That's the gap between Eastern and Pacific time.
Starts June at 6 opening, ends Aug 5 in the evening. This explains 6, 9 and 13. check_ins were stamped 3 hours
late, so evening check_ins looked like they were after closing, and check_ins landed after their own check_out,
which broke the pairing. 
I was wrong earlier when I said both turnstiles were closing events, and that the visits couldn't be recovered.
Fix in the report: use ingested_at when event_ts is later than it.
