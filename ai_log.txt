Report decisions

a check_out at the same branch".

because the query has to run on a different database.

check_outs were the test cards, and that the same-day rule only affects Mission Bay in summer.
- After the clock problem showed up, AI suggested using instead_at when event_ts is later than it. I agreed
because it fixes Mission Bay without naming a device or a 3 hour offset.
